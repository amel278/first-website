Funfact About Snow

Snow it is not actually white. Because they are made of water, snowflakes are naturally clear or transparent. Snow only appears white because the surfaces of the stacked ice crystals reflect the entire spectrum of sunlight in various directions.

Definition and Characteristic about Snow

About Description
Definition Snow consists of a collection of tiny ice crystals formed when water vapor in the air freezes directly at temperatures below 0°C.
Shape Ice crystals have a hexagonal structure due to the unique bonding of water molecules.
Color Snow appears white because its surface reflects the entire spectrum of sunlight that strikes it.
